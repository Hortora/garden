## Symptom

References to "the engine" or "engine/" during cross-repo design work are ambiguous — it's unclear whether the speaker means soredium's `engine/` subdirectory or the `Hortora/engine` repository.

## Context

Soredium contains an `engine/` subdirectory (`io.hortora.garden:engine`) — a Quarkus picocli CLI for garden deduplication, feature extraction, and pattern detection. The `Hortora/engine` repository (`io.hortora:engine`) is a separate Quarkus application providing the garden retrieval engine — MCP server, search, Qdrant, federation, provenance, outcome tracking. Both share the `io.hortora` group, both are Quarkus, both deal with garden entries.

## Root cause

The name "engine" was independently chosen for two related but distinct artifacts: one is a CLI tool (soredium's), the other is a running service (Hortora's). The overlapping domain (garden operations) and technology stack (Quarkus/Java) compound the confusion.

## Why this is non-obvious

Both projects appear in the same workspace during cross-repo work. A developer joining a design discussion who sees `engine/` in soredium and `Hortora/engine` in the org will assume they're the same thing — or that one is a submodule of the other. The Maven coordinates (`io.hortora.garden:engine` vs `io.hortora:engine`) are close enough to reinforce the confusion.
