### Context
`DescriptorCollector.deriveDispositionAxes()` projects a weighted `dispositionProfile` (e.g. Jungian cognitive functions) onto the 5 disposition axes via cross-vocabulary mapping. This requires a registered vocabulary URI.

### Behaviour
When `AgentDisposition.dispositionProfile()` is populated but the descriptor has no `dispositionVocabulary` (and no `domainVocabulary`), or the URI is present but not registered in `VocabularyRegistry`, the method returns the descriptor unchanged. No error, no warning, no log line. The profile is preserved on the descriptor, but all 5 axes (`socialOrient`, `ruleFollowing`, `riskAppetite`, `autonomy`, `conflictMode`) remain empty.

### Three conditions required for derivation to fire
1. `dispositionVocabulary` (or fallback `domainVocabulary`) must be non-null and non-blank
2. The URI must be registered in `VocabularyRegistry` (`isRegistered(vocabUri)` returns true)
3. `dispositionProfile` must be non-null and non-empty

If any condition fails, the method returns silently. Condition 1 is the most commonly missed — YAML descriptors with `mbtiType` but no `dispositionVocabulary: urn:casehub:vocab:jungian` produce profiles with empty axes.

### Why this is non-obvious
A developer adding `mbtiType: ENTJ` to a YAML descriptor expects the 5 axes to auto-populate. The `dispositionVocabulary` field is not enforced by the YAML parser or any validation step — its absence causes silent degradation rather than a clear error.
